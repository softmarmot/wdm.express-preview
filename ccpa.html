California Residents

Your privacy rights under the CCPA/CPRA

If you are a California resident, including a job applicant or employee of WDM EXPRESS LLC, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have collected about you and give you a copy
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions

We do not sell or share your personal information, and our Website does not use cookies or tracking for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.

How to make a request

  • Email: dispatch@wdm.express
  • Phone: +1 (440) 709-1601

Please include your name and how you have interacted with us (for example, a quote request or a job application) so we can verify your identity. You may also use an authorized agent with your written permission. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. There may be situations where we cannot honor a request because of an exception in the CCPA/CPRA or another legal requirement (for example, driver records we must keep under DOT/FMCSA rules); if so, we will explain why. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

For full details, see the Privacy Notice for California Residents in our Privacy Policy.
