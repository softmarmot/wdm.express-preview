Privacy Policy

WDM EXPRESS LLC · USDOT 4102303 · MC 1565080 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

WDM EXPRESS LLC ("WDM Express", "Company", "we", "us" or "our") is an asset-based motor carrier based in Grafton, Ohio. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, who we disclose it to, and the choices you have.

This policy applies to information we collect through our website wdm.express (the "Website"), through the forms on it, and in phone calls, emails and text messages between you and us. It also explains, in general terms, the information we handle about drivers, job applicants and employees. It does not apply to websites or services of third parties, even if our Website links to them.

By using the Website or sending us your information, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

Children Under the Age of 16

Our Website and services are intended for businesses and adults. They are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we learn that we have received such information, we will delete it. If you believe we may have information from or about a child under 16, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Shipping (quote) requests: your name, company, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery locations, ready date, equipment type and cargo details.
  • Driver job applications: your name, phone number, email address, city or location, the position you are interested in, driving experience, preferred schedule, possible start date, whether your driving record has major violations in the last 3 years, and any message you add (for example, endorsements or equipment experience).
  • Your consents: whether you agreed to be contacted about your application and whether you agreed to receive text messages, with the date of your submission.
  • Communications: records and copies of your calls, emails and text messages with us, including your contact details and what you tell us.

Information collected automatically

Our Website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, web beacons or any other tracking technologies, and it does not show targeted advertising. Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers automatically receive technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time. This information is used to deliver and secure the Website, not to profile visitors. If we ever add analytics or similar tools, we will update this policy first.

Information about drivers, applicants and employees

If you apply to drive for us or work for us, the hiring and employment process (which happens outside the Website) may involve additional information that federal and state law requires a motor carrier to collect and keep, such as:

  • Government identifiers, including driver's license or CDL details, Social Security number and work-eligibility documents
  • Employment history, previous-employer safety performance history, and motor vehicle records
  • DOT medical examiner's certificate and drug and alcohol testing results, as required by DOT/FMCSA regulations
  • Payroll, tax and bank account information needed to pay you
  • Location and driving data from electronic logging devices (ELDs) and vehicle tracking, while a driver is on duty
  • Information about race, ethnicity, sex or age only where required by law or voluntarily provided

We use this information only for hiring, employment, payroll, safety and legal compliance, and we never sell it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote requests and questions, and to provide transportation services
  • To coordinate pickups and deliveries, and to communicate with shippers, brokers, receivers and drivers about shipments, dispatch and schedules
  • To review job applications, contact applicants, hire drivers and manage employment
  • To send text messages to people who agreed to receive them (see "Text Messaging (SMS) Communications" below)
  • To operate, maintain and secure our Website and business
  • To comply with laws and regulations (including DOT/FMCSA requirements), respond to lawful requests, and protect our rights, our people and the public
  • For any other purpose we describe when you provide the information, or with your consent

How We Disclose Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. We disclose personal information only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run our business and may process information only on our behalf, including:
    • Web3Forms, which delivers Website form submissions to our email
    • Google Workspace, our email provider
    • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Website
    • Our phone and text messaging providers, and accounting, payroll and other business software providers
  • Parties to a shipment, such as the shipper, broker or receiver of a load, only as needed to complete that shipment
  • Background-check, drug-testing and similar providers, and previous employers, as part of hiring drivers as required by law
  • Insurers, government agencies and law enforcement (for example, the DOT and FMCSA), when required by law, legal process or regulation, or when needed to protect safety or our legal rights
  • Companies under common ownership or control with us, if any now or in the future, only for the purposes described in this policy
  • A buyer or successor in the event of a merger, sale of assets, reorganization or similar transaction, subject to this policy
  • Anyone else with your consent

Text Messaging (SMS) Communications

WDM Express may send text messages to brokers, shippers, receivers, drivers and employees who provide their mobile phone number to us and agree to receive texts — for example, by checking the SMS consent box on a form on our website — for purposes related to shipments, dispatch and work coordination. Messages are sent individually by our staff. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase, service or employment.

  • Message frequency varies depending on your shipments or work schedule.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. After you reply STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and no further texts.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the information below.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with any third parties.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Quote requests and business correspondence are kept for as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business relationship and records. Driver and employee records are kept for the periods required by DOT/FMCSA and other laws, plus any period needed for legal claims, tax, insurance or accident matters.

Your Choices and Rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep by law). You can also ask us to stop contacting you. To make a request, email dispatch@wdm.express or call +1 (440) 709-1601. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Residents of some states may have additional rights under their state's law. California residents, see the notice below.
