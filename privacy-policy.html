Privacy Policy

WDM EXPRESS LLC · USDOT 4102303 · MC 1565080 · Effective date: October 20, 2026

WDM EXPRESS LLC ("WDM Express", "we", "us", "our") is a transportation company providing freight and delivery services in the United States and Canada. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how we handle text message (SMS) communications.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide to us directly when you request our services, submit a form on our website, arrange or book a shipment, apply for a job, or contact us. This may include:

  • Name, company name and job title
  • Phone number (including mobile phone number) and email address
  • Business address, pickup and delivery addresses
  • Shipment details such as load numbers, appointment times and delivery documents
  • For drivers and job applicants: information provided during hiring, such as contact details, license and employment history

Our website may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, to keep the site working properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide transportation and delivery services and to coordinate pickups and deliveries
  • To communicate with brokers, shippers, receivers, drivers and employees about shipments, dispatch and schedules
  • To send shipment status updates, delivery confirmations and proof of delivery
  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To process job applications and manage our relationship with employees and drivers
  • To meet legal, safety, insurance and regulatory requirements

3. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share information only when necessary to provide our services or when required by law:

  • With parties involved in a specific shipment (for example, the broker, shipper or receiver of that load), only to the extent needed to complete the delivery
  • With service providers that help us operate our business (such as phone, messaging, website form, software and accounting providers), who may use the information only to perform services for us
  • With government authorities, insurers or law enforcement when required by law or to protect safety and our legal rights

4. Text Messaging (SMS) Communications

WDM Express may send text messages to brokers, shippers, receivers, drivers and employees who provide their mobile phone number to us and agree to receive texts — for example, by checking the SMS consent box on a form on our website — for purposes related to shipments, dispatch and work coordination. Messages are sent individually by our staff. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase, service or employment.

  • Message frequency varies depending on your shipments or work schedule.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. After you reply STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and no further texts.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the information below.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with any third parties.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your information. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

6. Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by contacting us using the details below. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
