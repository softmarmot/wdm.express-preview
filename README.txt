WDM Express LLC — website (October 2026 update)

PUBLISH: upload the contents of this folder to the public directory of your hosting (wdm.express). No build step.

TURN ON EMAIL DELIVERY OF FORMS (2 minutes, free):
1. Open https://web3forms.com, enter the company email where requests should arrive, get the Access Key.
2. Open app.js, find  accessKey:''  at the top and paste the key between the quotes.
3. Upload app.js again. Both forms (shipping request + driver application) now arrive by email.
Until the key is added, the forms still work: visitors get "Send by text" and "Call" buttons with their details prefilled.
