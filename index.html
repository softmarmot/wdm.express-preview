01 / ABOUT WDM EXPRESS

Small fleet.
Big accountability.

WDM Express LLC is an asset-based carrier headquartered in Grafton, Ohio, running under its own authority since 2023. Our fleet of compact box trucks, full-size International and Freightliner box trucks and a Volvo tractor is driven by our own team — your load is never handed off to someone you have never heard of.

That means you deal with the people who actually move your freight: one dispatcher, straight answers and updates from pickup to proof of delivery. Shippers, brokers and growing businesses call us when a load simply has to arrive on time.

07 / FAQ

Before
the journey.

The details make the difference.

Are you a carrier or a broker?+

We’re an asset-based carrier with our own trucks and drivers — USDOT 4102303, MC 1565080. You can check our authority, insurance and safety record on FMCSA SAFER.

Which vehicle is right for my shipment?+

That depends on cargo dimensions, total weight, loading access and route. Include these details in your request, or select “Help me choose.” Final capacity and availability must be confirmed before booking.

What do I need to prepare for a quote?+

Pickup and delivery cities or ZIP codes, preferred dates, cargo description, dimensions and total weight. Include pallet count, dock access and any special loading or delivery requirements.

Do you handle time-sensitive shipments?+

Yes — it’s a big part of what we do. Give us the earliest pickup time and the hard delivery deadline. If the load needs to move today, call dispatch at +1 (440) 709-1601.

Where do you operate?+

We haul interstate freight across the USA from our base in Grafton, Ohio. Cross-border loads to Canada are possible — ask us before booking.

What insurance do you carry?+

Our $750,000 auto liability coverage is on file with FMCSA. Ask us for a certificate of insurance (COI) and cargo coverage details for your specific load.

Does sending a request book a truck?+

No. A shipment is booked only after WDM Express confirms the equipment, schedule, rate and terms with you — by phone and a rate confirmation.
